P. bolivari, P. ornatus* (SA/mesophilous); *A. gestroi, A. granulatum, A. depressum*; *Cubaris

*Porcellionides floria*. *Ligia* spp. (Diplocheta) = textbook **LI**. **No terrestrial isopod is
genuinely arboreal (AR)** — corticolous climbers like *P. scaber* are the closest.

## Axis 2 — Trophic guild

**Baseline:** virtually all Oniscidea are general detritivores/saprophages on decaying litter, wood
and microbial biofilm; most facultatively coprophagous, take carrion/protein. Lignocellulose
digestion is mediated by gut/hepatopancreatic symbionts — *Candidatus* Hepatoplasma crinochetorum
(Mollicutes) & *Ca.* Hepatincola porcellionum (Wang, Brune & Zimmer 2004, 2007). **Copper**
(haemocyanin) and **calcium** (cuticle) are central; coprophagy re-inoculates gut microbiota and
recovers copper (near-obligate). **Xylophagy sensu stricto does NOT occur** — terrestrial isopods
are wood-*associated*, not obligate wood-borers (true xylophagy is marine: *Limnoria*/*Sphaeroma*).

## Axis 3 — Life-history & reproduction

**Universal:** direct development in a **marsupium** (oostegite brood pouch); no free larva; young
emerge as **mancae** (~3 manca stages), miniature adults lacking the last pereopod pair. Ancestral
sex determination in Crinocheta is female-heterogametic (ZW/ZZ), heavily modifiable by
*Wolbachia*/f-element.

**Sociality / dimorphism:** *Hemilepistus reaumuri* is the textbook subsociality case (Linsenmair
1984, 1987, 2007) — obligate biparental care, monogamous pair bonds, family chemical "badge,"
division of labour, semelparity; the highest sociality grade in isopods. Some *Porcellio* (e.g.
*P. albinus*) show convergent burrow-based social behaviour. Males of *Porcellio*/*Porcellionides*
have longer spear-shaped uropods & modified copulatory pleopods; *A. vulgare* shows female-biased
size-at-age and multiple paternity (up to 7 fathers/brood, Valette et al. 2017).

## Caveats & explicit non-verifications

- **"Rubber Ducky" origin conflict:** vendors disagree (Thailand vs Vietnam limestone caves);
  formally undescribed either way — unresolved, not adjudicated.
- ***Porcellio laevis* brood size:** two primary figures (~66 vs ~21 mancae) from different
  populations (Warburg et al. 2001 vs Nair 1976) — both reported, not averaged.
- **Genuinely unstudied (class c):** *Platyarthrus hoffmannseggii* / *Venezillo parvus* / *Nagurus*
  spp. / *Merulanella* spp. life-history & feeding — do **not** populate from congeners without a flag.
- **Nomenclatural trap:** *Porcellio hoffmannseggi* ≠ *Platyarthrus hoffmannseggii* (both abbreviate
  "*P. hoffmannseggii*").
- **No fabricated citations:** where an attribution could not be confirmed it is flagged, not asserted.
