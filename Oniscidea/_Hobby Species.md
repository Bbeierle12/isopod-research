# Hobby & Cultured Isopods

Terrestrial isopod species that are **kept in the pet / bioactive-terrarium hobby** and are present in this vault as validly described species. **48 species** across 15 genera.

> [!warning] Undescribed trade forms are not listed here
> Much of the live trade is *undescribed* species sold under trade names (e.g. *Cubaris* "Rubber Ducky", *Merulanella* "Red Devil", many *Cubaris* sp.). Those have no accepted scientific name, so they have no note in this taxonomy-based vault. Add them manually as free notes if you want to track them.

Every species below has `in_culture: yes` in its frontmatter — query with `tag:#hobby` or Dataview `WHERE in_culture = "yes"` to regenerate this view.
